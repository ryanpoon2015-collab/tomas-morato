Welcome to Tomas Morato Avenue

Tomas Morato Avenue is the culinary beating heart of Quezon City, Philippines. Spanning blocks of high-energy bistros, cozy third-wave cafes, date-night spots, and after-hours restobars, this iconic food district is where flavor meets culture.

Tomas Morato Discovery was built to help food lovers navigate this rich gastronomic ecosystem. Whether you are seeking a specific ambiance, dietary preference, or just feeling completely indecisive, we've got you covered.
